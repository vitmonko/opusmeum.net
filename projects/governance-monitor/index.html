17 checks in six areas

Each check has a threshold and a severity you set. Every site gets a health score from 0 to 100, so you can see the trend month by month.

  • 3 checks

    Finds sites without owners

    One owner or none, and owners whose accounts are disabled.

  • 2 checks

    Flags inactive sites

    Sites and teams with no activity for 90, 180 or 365 days.

  • 3 checks

    Lists external sharing

    Guests, Anyone links and sites shared wider than your policy.

  • 3 checks

    Shows overshared sites

    Sites open to Everyone except external users, found before Copilot surfaces them.

  • 3 checks

    Ranks storage use

    Sites near quota, libraries over 5,000 items and high version limits.

  • 3 checks

    Checks site hygiene

    Naming rules, missing sensitivity labels and sites without a description.

Site owners confirm what stays

Every quarter, each owner gets a Teams card for the sites they own: keep, archive or fix sharing. If nobody answers within 14 days, it goes to their manager.

From the dashboard, admins can set a site to read-only, add a backup owner or remove broad access. Every action is logged.

Changes, deadlines and exceptions

Every scan is compared with the last one, and every finding gets an owner and a due date.

  • Shows permission changes

    New guests, groups and broad grants on each site since the last scan.

  • Tracks fix deadlines

    Due dates by severity, with reminders to the owner and then their manager.

  • Expires risk exceptions

    Each exception has an approver and an end date. Then the finding reopens.

  • Splits business and technical owners

    Business owners confirm a site is needed. Technical owners fix its settings.
