What we build

Most organizations already license the tools below through Microsoft 365. We help you use them properly: one platform, one set of permissions, and nothing your IT team can't support.

  • Power AppsBusiness applications

    Apps that replace spreadsheets and paper forms: inspections, approvals, asset tracking, onboarding, field work. Canvas and model-driven apps on Dataverse or SharePoint, working on desktop and mobile.

  • Power AutomateWorkflow automation

    Flows that move data between Outlook, Teams, SharePoint and your line-of-business systems, plus desktop automation for older applications that have no API.

  • Power BIReporting and analytics

    Data models and reports people actually open. We connect your sources, define each metric once, and set up scheduled refresh and row-level security.

  • SharePointIntranet and documents

    Intranets, document libraries and metadata that make files easy to find, with permissions that match how your organization actually works.

AI that works inside your processes

We add AI where it removes real work, using Microsoft's AI stack, so your data stays in your tenant under the security and compliance rules you already have.

Copilot Studio agents

Agents for employees or customers that answer from your SharePoint, policies and knowledge bases, and can take action: log a ticket, start an approval, update a record.

Document processing

Read invoices, contracts, receipts and forms with AI Builder and Azure AI Document Intelligence, then route the extracted data through a flow with a human review step.

Azure OpenAI solutions

Summarizing, classifying, drafting and searching across your own content, built on Azure OpenAI with retrieval from your data rather than the public internet.

Microsoft 365 Copilot rollout

Get value from Copilot licenses: readiness checks on permissions and data, use-case workshops with each team, practical prompt guides and adoption tracking.

AI in apps and reports

Copilot inside Power Apps, natural-language questions over Power BI reports, and AI prompts in flows that sort emails, tag requests and flag exceptions.

Governance and security

Environment strategy, data loss prevention policies, sensitivity labels and usage guidelines, so AI can only reach the data each person is allowed to see.

Not sure where AI fits? Start with one process that takes too long. In a free call we'll tell you honestly whether AI helps, or whether a simple flow does the job.

How a project runs

Small steps, visible progress, and a working version early, so you are reacting to something real rather than a specification.

  1. Discover

    A free first call, then a short workshop to map the process, the people involved and where the data lives.

  2. Prototype

    A clickable first version your team can try, so scope and priorities are settled before the main build.

  3. Build

    Delivery in short cycles, tested with the people who will use it, with licensing and security checked along the way.

  4. Hand over

    Documentation, training and admin guides. Ongoing support if you want it, full independence if you don't.
