On 5 November 2026, Microsoft announced that Claude models by Anthropic in Microsoft Copilot are coming into the EU Data Boundary. Organizations in the EU and the European Free Trade Association (EFTA) will be able to use Claude across Copilot experiences, including Copilot Cowork, with processing inside the EU Data Boundary, where their data is already stored. Microsoft targets the end of 2026 and says it will share more details closer to availability.

## What is new

- **Processing moves into the EU Data Boundary.** Claude will join the Microsoft-operated models that already process data inside the boundary. Today Claude powers options in Chat, Copilot in Microsoft 365 apps, Copilot Cowork and Copilot Code.
- **No action for current users.** Organizations already using Claude in Copilot do not need to do anything. Processing moves automatically once it is available.
- **Still off by default.** In the EU and EFTA, the Claude setting stays disabled by default. Admins can enable it for all users or for selected users and groups in the Microsoft 365 admin center.
- **Same data protection.** Claude in Copilot is covered by Microsoft's enterprise data protection, and customer data is not used to train foundation models.

## What has not changed yet

Until availability, Microsoft Learn still states that Claude models in Copilot, Researcher, Copilot Studio, Power Platform and Copilot in Microsoft 365 apps are currently excluded from the EU Data Boundary. The announcement names Copilot experiences, including Cowork, but does not say whether Copilot Studio and Power Platform are included. Treat those as excluded until Microsoft confirms otherwise.

## Why it matters

For many European organizations, the boundary exclusion was the reason to leave Claude off, and with it the choice of model in tools like Researcher and Cowork. Once processing stays inside the boundary, model choice becomes a business decision rather than a compliance blocker. Your data protection assessment still needs updating, but the conversation becomes much simpler.

## How to prepare

1. **Check your current setting.** In the Microsoft 365 admin center, go to Copilot, Settings, View all, then **AI providers operating as Microsoft subprocessors**.
2. **Update your data protection records.** Note today's transfer and the planned change, so the decision to enable Claude is documented either way.
3. **Prepare a pilot group.** Choose the users and groups who would benefit most, and enable Claude for them when your policy allows it.
4. **Review Copilot Studio separately.** Anthropic models in Copilot Studio and Power Platform have their own controls in the Power Platform admin center.
5. **Watch for the availability notice** before relying on the new commitment.
